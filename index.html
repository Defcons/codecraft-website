About Us

Software engineering for the energy sector

Codecraft AS is a Norwegian software engineering consultancy founded by Martin Davidsen, a Senior Software Engineer specializing in the oil & gas and energy industries. Our core expertise is building the software that runs subsea production systems, offshore platforms, and critical infrastructure — PLC control logic, SCADA interfaces, communication protocols, and custom applications.

Based in Rogaland — the heart of Norway's energy sector — we combine deep domain knowledge in automation with modern software development practices and AI-augmented workflows. With a Bachelor's in Maritime Electrical Automation, certified electrician credentials, and years of offshore commissioning experience, we understand the full stack from field devices to enterprise systems.
